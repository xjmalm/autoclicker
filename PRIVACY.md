# FigAutoClicker 隐私政策

**生效日期：2026 年 10 月 4 日**

FigAutoClicker（以下简称“本应用”）是一个离线运行的 Windows 鼠标自动化工具。本政策说明本应用如何处理您的信息。

## 1. 我们收集的信息

无。本应用不需要注册或登录，不收集、不存储、不传输任何个人信息。

本应用不会读取您的文档、剪贴板、浏览记录、通讯录、位置或任何文件内容，也不会收集设备标识符、使用统计或崩溃数据。

## 2. 网络访问与第三方共享

本应用不联网，不会向任何服务器发送数据，也不会与任何第三方共享、出售或披露您的信息。

## 3. 保存在您设备上的内容

为了让下次启动时沿用您的设置，本应用会在您的设备上保存一个配置文件：

- 路径：`%APPDATA%\FigAutoClicker\settings.json`
- 内容：鼠标按键、点击方式、点击间隔、重复方式、全局快捷键等界面选项
- 用途：仅用于恢复上次使用的设置

该文件始终保存在您的设备上，不会上传到任何地方。您可以随时删除它以重置设置；卸载本应用时，该文件会随应用数据一并清除。

## 4. 权限说明

本应用以完全信任（full trust）方式运行，用于实现两项核心功能：

- 通过 `RegisterHotKey` 注册全局快捷键（F8 / F9 / F10），使您在其他窗口获得焦点时也能开始或停止点击；
- 通过 `SendInput` 模拟鼠标点击。

本应用不会借助这些能力读取、记录或上传您的任何数据，也不会自动启动或修改其他应用程序。

## 5. 儿童隐私

本应用不收集任何人的信息，因此也不会收集儿童的个人信息。

## 6. 本政策的变更

如果本应用新增了会影响信息处理的功能，我们会在发布新版本时同步更新本政策，并更新上方的生效日期。

## 7. 联系方式

如对本政策有疑问，请联系：`xjmalm@hotmail.com`

# FigAutoClicker Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 4, 2026**

FigAutoClicker ("the app") is an offline Windows mouse automation utility. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

## 1. Information we collect

None. The app requires no account or sign-in, and it does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

The app does not read your documents, clipboard, browsing history, contacts, location, or file contents, and it does not collect device identifiers, usage statistics, or crash reports.

## 2. Network access and third parties

The app does not connect to the internet, does not send data to any server, and does not share, sell, or disclose your information to any third party.

## 3. Data stored on your device

To remember your preferences between sessions, the app stores a single configuration file on your device:

- Path: `%APPDATA%\FigAutoClicker\settings.json`
- Contents: interface options such as mouse button, click mode, click interval, repeat mode, and global hotkeys
- Purpose: to restore your last used settings

This file always stays on your device and is never uploaded anywhere. You can delete it at any time to reset your settings, and it is removed together with the app's data when you uninstall the app.

## 4. Permissions

The app runs as a full-trust desktop application in order to provide two core features:

- Registering global hotkeys (F8 / F9 / F10) with `RegisterHotKey`, so you can start and stop clicking while another window has focus;
- Simulating mouse clicks with `SendInput`.

These capabilities are not used to read, record, or upload any of your data, and the app never starts automatically and never modifies other applications.

## 5. Children's privacy

The app collects no information from anyone, and therefore collects no personal information from children.

## 6. Changes to this policy

If a new version of the app adds functionality that affects how information is handled, we will update this policy together with that release and update the effective date above.

If you have questions about this policy, contact: `xjmalm@hotmail.com`
